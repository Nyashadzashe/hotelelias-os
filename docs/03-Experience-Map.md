# Hotel Elias
## Experience Map

Version 1.0

Purpose:
Define the complete journey a visitor takes through the Hotel Elias universe.

## Design Philosophy
The Hotel Elias website should never feel like browsing a website. It should feel like arriving somewhere. Every interaction should be intentional, calm and memorable. The experience values atmosphere over speed, but never sacrifices usability. Technology should disappear behind the experience.

## Experience Principles
1. Arrival should feel special.
2. Navigation should feel effortless.
3. Every animation should have a purpose.
4. Visitors should always know where they are.
5. Music is the center of the experience.
6. The website should reward curiosity.
7. Simplicity is luxury.

## Before Designing Anything
I think the **hotel theme should be strongest at the beginning**, then gradually fade into the background.

For example:
- The arrival, check-in, room key, and lobby establish the world.
- Once you're in **Music**, **Videos**, or **Merch**, those sections should feel clean and premium—not like they're forcing hotel metaphors everywhere.
That balance keeps the concept memorable without becoming a gimmick.
After this document, the next logical step is **`04-Information-Architecture.md`**, where we define every page, route, and piece of content the site will contain. That blueprint will guide both the design and the development.
