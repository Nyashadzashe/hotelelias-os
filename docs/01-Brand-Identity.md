# Hotel Elias
## Brand Identity

Version 1.0

## Brand Overview

Hotel Elias is a cinematic music project that blends music, storytelling and technology into one immersive experience.
Rather than existing only as an artist, Hotel Elias exists as a world that listeners can enter.
Every song, visual, photograph, performance and digital interaction should feel like it belongs to the same universe.
The goal is to create timeless experiences that people remember long after they leave.

## Brand Positioning

Hotel Elias is an Alternative Rap artist who creates cinematic, atmospheric music through confident rap delivery, melodic songwriting and timeless visual storytelling.
The Hotel Elias experience blends music, design and technology into a single world built around quiet luxury, nostalgia and intentional craftsmanship.
